Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Second Chance Shelter handles the personal data you send us through the forms on this website. The data controller is Second Chance Shelter, Example Street 1, 00100 Helsinki, info@secondchanceshelter.com.

What Data We Collect

We only collect the data you enter in our forms:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the subject and your message.
  • Adoption application: your name, email address, phone number, home address, a description of your home and your message.

The website does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Why We Collect It

We use the data from the contact form only to answer your message. We use the data from the adoption application to assess whether the animal would suit your home, to contact you and to arrange a meeting. We do not share your data with anyone else or use it for marketing.

Where the Data Is Stored

The forms are sent through Formspree, an external form service that forwards the data to the shelter's email. The data is stored in Formspree and in the shelter's email. The map on the Contact page is provided by Google Maps, which may use its own cookies.

How Long We Keep It

Messages are deleted at the latest 12 months after they have been answered. Adoption applications are deleted at the latest 12 months after the adoption has been completed or the application has been declined.

Your Rights

You have the right to see the data we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete it. Send your request to info@secondchanceshelter.com. If you think your data has been handled incorrectly, you can contact the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland.
