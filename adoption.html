Adoption Process

Giving an animal a new home is a big decision. We want to make sure every animal finds a safe, loving and suitable home. Here is how our adoption process works.

How to Adopt

1. Find Your New Friend

Browse the dogs and cats on our Animals page. Read their descriptions and think about which animal would fit your lifestyle and home.

Meet the animals

2. Contact Us

When you find an animal you are interested in, send us a message. Tell us a little about yourself and why you would like to adopt.

Contact us

3. Meet the Animal

We will arrange a meeting at the shelter. This gives you a chance to get to know the animal and see how you connect with each other.

4. Adoption Discussion

We will discuss your home, daily routine and previous experience with animals. We also explain the animal's needs, personality and care requirements.

5. Welcome Home!

Once everything is agreed, we complete the adoption paperwork and go through the final details. Your new family member can then start settling into their new home.

Before You Adopt

Adopting an animal is a long-term commitment. Before applying, please consider the following:

  • Do you have enough time for daily care, exercise and attention?
  • Can you afford food, supplies and veterinary expenses?
  • Is your home suitable for the animal?
  • Are all members of your household ready for a new pet?
  • Are you prepared to care for the animal throughout its life?

Ready to Adopt?

Take a look at our available animals and find your new best friend. If you have any questions, our team is happy to help.
