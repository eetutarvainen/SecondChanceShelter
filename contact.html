Found a Homeless Animal?

  1. Check whether the animal has a collar or an ID tag.
  2. Take a photo of the animal and write down where and when you found it.
  3. Call us at +358 40 123 4567. Phone is the fastest way to reach us.
  4. If you cannot call, send us a message through the contact form above.
